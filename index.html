Install in 4 easy steps

No technical skills needed. Takes less than a minute.

  1. Download — Click here to download the extension
  2. Unzip the file, then open the chrome-extension folder inside it
  3. Open Chrome and type chrome://extensions in the address bar
  4. Turn on Developer mode (top-right switch) → click Load unpacked → select the chrome-extension folder
That's it! The dassistant icon appears in your Chrome toolbar. You get 5 free analyses automatically. Click the icon anytime to see your license key.
