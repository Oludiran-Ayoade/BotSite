How to install

Two ways to get the extension. Pick whichever is easiest for you.

Option 1 — Chrome Web Store (recommended)

1
Click "Add to Chrome" at the top of this page.
2
Click "Add extension" in the Chrome popup that appears.
3
Click the dassistant icon in your toolbar, enter your email, and start analyzing.
Option 2 — Manual install (for developers)
1
Download the extension ZIP
Download (.zip)
2
Unzip the file. Inside you'll find a chrome-extension folder.
3
Go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the folder.
That's it! The dassistant icon appears in your Chrome toolbar. Click it, enter your email, and you get 4 free analyses instantly. The green Analyze pill will appear on your current page.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is it free to try? ⌄
Yes. You get 4 free analyses when you sign up. No credit card required. The free trial lasts 30 days.
Does it work on Firefox or Safari? ⌄
Currently it's Chrome-only. It works on any computer that runs Chrome, including Mac, Windows, and Linux. You can install it directly from the Chrome Web Store.
Is my screen data stored? ⌄
No. Screen captures are sent to our AI for analysis and immediately discarded. We never store your screen recordings.
Can I cancel my subscription? ⌄
Yes, cancel anytime. Your credits remain available until your current billing period ends.
What if I run out of credits mid-month? ⌄
Buy a one-time credit pack ($2 for 50, $5 for 200, or $10 for 500 analyses). No subscription needed — credits are added instantly.
How fast is the analysis? ⌄
Most analyses complete in 5-10 seconds. The extension captures what's on your screen, sends it to our AI, and displays the answer in a compact overlay.
