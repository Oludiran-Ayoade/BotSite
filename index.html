How to install

Two ways to get the extension. Pick whichever is easiest for you.

Option 1 — Chrome Web Store (recommended)

1
Click "Add to Chrome" at the top of this page.
2
Click "Add extension" in the Chrome popup that appears.
3
Click the Assistant icon in your toolbar, enter your email, and start analyzing.
Option 2 — Manual install (for developers) ▾
1
Download the extension ZIP
Download (.zip)
2
Unzip the file. Inside you'll find a chrome-extension folder.
3
Go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the folder.
That's it! The Assistant icon appears in your Chrome toolbar. Click it, enter your email, and you get 5 free analyses instantly. The green Analyze pill will appear on your current page.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is it free to try?

Yes. You get 5 free analyses (15 credits) when you sign up. No credit card required. The free trial lasts 30 days.

Does it work on Firefox or Safari?

Currently it's Chrome-only. It works on any computer that runs Chrome, including Mac, Windows, and Linux. You can install it directly from the Chrome Web Store.

Is my screen data stored?

No. Short clips are sent to our AI for analysis and immediately discarded. We never store your screen recordings.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, cancel anytime. Your credits remain available until your current billing period ends.

What if I run out of credits mid-month?

Buy a one-time credit pack ($5 for 1,000 or $15 for 4,000 analyses). No subscription needed — credits are added instantly.

How fast is the analysis?

Most analyses complete in 5-10 seconds. The extension records a 5-second clip, sends it to our AI, and displays the answer in a compact overlay.
